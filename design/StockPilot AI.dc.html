How your data is handled

Minimal data. No brokerage link, no SSN or PAN. Paper portfolio only.
Model routing. Anything with your profile, portfolio or chat uses Standard mode, which Meta does not train on. Contributor mode is used only for public filings.
Isolation. Row-level security restricts every table to your user ID.
Encryption. At rest and in transit.
Audit. Every proposal, approval, profile change and model call has a trace ID.
