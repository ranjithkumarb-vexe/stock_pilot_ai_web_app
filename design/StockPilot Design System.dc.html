Identity and motion

StockPilot is a flight instrument for research. The visual language borrows from cockpit displays: a graphite panel, one signal-lime accent, mono readouts and precise motion.

The mark: Verified Heading
Brackets are a citation: every claim has a source.
The check is verification against XBRL.
The rising stroke ends in a waypoint: the heading you set with your rules.
32-unit grid. Bracket stroke 2.6, check stroke 3, waypoint radius 2.7. Minimum size 16 px. Keep clear space of a quarter of the mark on all sides.
App icon and monochrome
Graphite tile is the primary app icon. Lime tile for marketing. Single-color versions for favicons, print and light backgrounds.

Motion system

Motion marks agent activity and state change. Everything moves on spring physics through the Motion engine (the Framer Motion core). There are no ease or linear transitions, and no spinning circles: thinking is shown with morphing shapes, shimmer and pulsing glow.

01Heading indicatorThe active nav marker springs between items, so moving through the app feels like changing course. Screens enter from the right going forward and from the left going back.
02Position radarDashboard instrument. Each holding is a blip whose distance from the centre is its weight. The dashed ring is your position cap. Breaching blips pulse. Click one to open its brief.
03Hold to approveApproving a paper trade takes a 0.9 s press-and-hold that fills the button, so a click cannot commit by accident. Enter or Space approves at once for keyboard users.
04Fill stampEvery simulated order ends with a stamp: the box and check draw in, then FILLED or QUEUED with side, size and price. It never blocks input.
05Pilot is readingOpening a brief shows the verification pipeline ticking through fetch, PromptGuard, XBRL match and personalization, then the metric tiles scan in.
06Command barPress ⌘K or Ctrl K to jump to a screen or ticker, run an action, or send a question to Pilot.
07Collapsing HUDOn scroll the top bar shrinks and its live readouts (value, today, rule health) stay pinned.
08Briefing revealThe daily briefing resolves word by word from blur, then the supporting points follow.
